HR Employee Attrition Dashboard

IBM HR Analytics dataset · 1,470 employees · answers: “Why are employees leaving?”

Why are employees leaving?

    Headline findings (all employees)

    Computed on the full dataset, independent of filters

    • Overtime is the strongest single signal: 30.5% of employees working overtime left vs 10.4% of those who don’t (≈3×).
    • Early-career and low-pay staff leave most: 35.8% attrition at ages 18–25; 28.6% below $3k monthly income vs 5.6% above $12k.
    • First year is the danger zone: 34.9% of people with ≤1 year at the company left.
    • Sales Representatives (39.8%) and single employees (25.5%) are the highest-risk groups; no stock options (24.4%) and frequent travel (24.9%) add risk.
    Attrition rate = leavers ÷ employees in the group. Dashed line = attrition rate of the current selection. Red bars are above that line, green below. Groups with fewer than 10 people are hidden. The dataset has no Region field, so Department, Age group, Job role, Marital status and Overtime are offered as filters instead.
